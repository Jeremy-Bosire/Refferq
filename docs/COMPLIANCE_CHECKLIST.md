# Kenya launch compliance checklist

This checklist is informational, not legal advice. Have Kenyan counsel and qualified finance/compliance advisers review the actual operating model before accepting customer funds or launching rewards.

## Business and financial model

- [ ] Confirm the operating entity, business registration details, beneficial ownership, and customer support contacts.
- [ ] Document which entity sells each product or service, who receives each payment, when a sale is recognized as revenue, and how refunds and chargebacks are funded.
- [ ] Obtain written Kenyan legal and accounting review of the proposed referral compensation structure, including its levels, funding source, and any rewards linked to customer deposits.
- [ ] Do not advertise deposit returns, yield, guaranteed income, or deposit-funded rewards until the model has been reviewed and explicitly approved. No such product behavior is enabled by this localization work.

## Payments and custody

- [ ] Ask Kenyan counsel whether the product activities require authorization under the National Payment System Act or other payment-services rules.
- [ ] Confirm with the chosen licensed PSP how customer funds are held, safeguarded, reconciled, refunded, and returned if the PSP relationship ends.
- [ ] Confirm whether any proposed holding or transfer of customer funds could be deposit-taking under the Banking Act and obtain advice before implementation.
- [ ] Complete Daraja sandbox integration and reconciliation before production credentials or customer funds are enabled.
- [ ] Agree provider settlement, callback security, reversal, dispute, and incident procedures in writing.

## Consumer protection and promotion

- [ ] Review customer terms, refund and withdrawal terms, fees, limits, reward eligibility, reward holds, and complaint handling for clarity and compliance.
- [ ] Review referral promotions under the Consumer Protection Act and Competition Act, including rules on pyramid schemes, misleading claims, and unfair practices.
- [ ] Prohibit spam, fake accounts, self-referrals, impersonation, and misleading earnings claims in the published referral agreement.
- [ ] Ensure all public claims and testimonials are genuine, substantiated, permissioned, and not presented as typical outcomes without evidence.

## Privacy, identity, and security

- [ ] Determine whether ODPC registration is required for the organization and relevant processing activities; complete it where required.
- [ ] Publish a privacy notice covering purposes, lawful bases, processors, cross-border transfers, retention, user rights, and contact details.
- [ ] Implement consent records, data access/export and deletion request handling, retention schedules, and processor agreements.
- [ ] Review KYC collection, identity encryption, access controls, masking, incident response, and data-breach notification procedures against the Data Protection Act 2019.
- [ ] Review security controls and incident procedures against the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act and applicable regulations.

## Tax, AML/CFT, and operations

- [ ] Obtain KRA advice on income recognition, VAT treatment, withholding obligations, invoicing, and annual reward statements. Configure rates only on documented professional advice.
- [ ] Define transaction-monitoring thresholds, escalation paths, record retention, suspicious-activity handling, and the responsible compliance officer with appropriate advisers.
- [ ] Train staff on account freezes, manual review, payout reversals, privacy-safe support, and audit-log access.
- [ ] Complete production readiness checks for payment provider credentials, DNS, email/SMS sender approvals, backups, monitoring, and disaster recovery.

## Required launch sign-offs

- [ ] Kenyan lawyer has reviewed the business, payments, referral, and consumer terms.
- [ ] PSP and customer-funds arrangement is documented and approved.
- [ ] Finance/compliance owners have signed off on ledger, reconciliation, refunds, taxes, and reporting.
- [ ] Daraja and any SMS provider production onboarding is complete.
- [ ] Privacy, security, and customer-support processes have been exercised.
